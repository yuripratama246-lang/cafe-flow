# Cafe Flow

Build a modern POS Kasir and QR Menu Digital web application for cafes and restaurants:
1. Dashboard: Daily revenue, total transactions, items sold, recent sales summary, and quick navigation shortcuts.
2. POS Kasir: Fast, touch/desktop-friendly cashier layout with categories, search, cart, discount & tax calculations, payment options (Cash with change calculation, Card, QRIS), and printable/viewable digital receipt.
3. Product & Inventory Management: Add/edit products (name, price, image, category, stock, QR menu toggle) and manage inventory with auto stock deduction on sales and low stock indicators.
4. Mobile-first QR Menu for Customers: Responsive customer view to browse menu items, build a cart, add order notes, and submit orders; plus a QR code generator for tables.
5. Order Management: Real-time order queue with status tracking (New, Preparing, Ready, Completed, Cancelled) receiving orders from both POS and QR menu.
6. Reports & Transactions: Transaction history, receipt details, date range filters, payment breakdown, and best-selling products.
7. Clean, modern SaaS POS design with ready-to-use sample F&B items and scalable Supabase-ready schema structure.
